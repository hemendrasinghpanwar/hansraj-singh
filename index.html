About

A coordinating voice between institutions and the people they serve

Hansraj Singh Rawat works at the intersection of policy coordination and multilateral diplomacy, serving as a liaison between institutional stakeholders and the Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC) system. His work centres on translating complex policy dialogue into coordinated, actionable outcomes.

With a background in international relations and public affairs, he has supported delegations, drafted institutional correspondence, and coordinated logistics for high-level sessions — always with an eye toward the practical follow-through that turns discussion into implementation.

He is driven by a belief that effective multilateralism depends as much on careful coordination as it does on policy itself.
